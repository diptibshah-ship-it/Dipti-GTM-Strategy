# Roamly Groups · Messaging Pillars & AI-Generated Asset, Module 4

## Fro/To Market Shift
- **From:** _(Group travel experiences are fragmented and organizer-heavy — one person discovers the activity, coordinates everyone in group chats, manages availability, collects payments, and keeps track of the booking.)_
- **To:** _(Group experiences are collaborative and easy — friends can plan, book, pay, and manage a shared local experience together in one place.)_

## Audience Segmentation
- **Number of audiences:** _(2)_
- **Justification:** _(Both audiences participate in the same booking journey but experience different pain points. The organizer feels the burden of coordination, while participants want convenience and visibility without taking over planning. Messaging should therefore lead with reducing the organizer's workload while showing participants how easy it is to join and contribute.)_

## Messaging Pillars

### Pillar, _(Plan Together)_
- **🎯 Value prop:** _(Turn group ideas into actual plans without endless coordination.)_
- **🏆 Capabilities:** _(Shared itinerary planning, coordinated availability, collaborative experience selection, and a single place for the group to see the plan.)_
- **📊 Evidence:** _(Roamly already sees customers attempting to coordinate group bookings across separate accounts, demonstrating existing demand for a shared planning experience.)_

## Activation Across Surfaces
- **_(Website / Landing Page:)_:** _(Lead with the emotional benefit: “Great trips shouldn't come with a designated group coordinator.” Follow with a simple explanation of how friends can discover experiences, plan together, and split payments.)_
- **_(In-Product:)_:** _(When Roamly detects behavior suggesting multiple travelers or group planning, introduce Groups contextually: “Planning with friends? Invite your group and book together.”)_
- **_(Social / Paid Campaign:)_:** _(Build creative around the relatable pain of being the “trip planner” — chasing friends for Venmo payments, sending screenshots, managing multiple chats — contrasted with one shared Roamly Groups experience.)_

## Blind Peer Read
- **Who they thought it's for:** _(Groups of friends traveling together, particularly the person who normally takes responsibility for organizing the group's activities.)_
- **What they thought it does:** _(Helps groups discover and plan local experiences together, coordinate the booking, and split payments without relying on multiple apps or separate accounts.)_
- **What made them trust it:** _(The emphasis on vetted local experts, Roamly's established experience marketplace, its network of 12,000+ experts across 40 cities, and its strong repeat-booking performance.)_
