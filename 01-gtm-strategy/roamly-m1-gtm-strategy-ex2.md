Existing Roamly travelers who frequently plan trips with friends or family and struggle with separate bookings, split payments, and coordination.

Roamly Groups makes group travel simple by combining booking, split payments, and shared itineraries in one place.

Expand — turn existing Roamly users into group organizers and bring their friends into Roamly.

Independent travelers planning experiences with friends or family.

Sharpen the messaging around the existing pain—not just the feature. Focus on eliminating group-trip coordination chaos.

PLG first, supported by community-led growth through travel communities, referrals, and social sharing.

10. Success metric

Group booking conversion rate — the percentage of group-intent users who complete a group booking.
