# Roamly Groups · Pricing & Packaging Recommendation, Module 5 (Exercise 1)

## Responses
- **Pricing goal (Where to win first):** _(Drive adoption and group-booking conversion first, while creating a path to monetize the increased booking value.)_
- **Why (Justification):** _(The biggest opportunity is getting existing Roamly users to choose Roamly for group trips. Pricing should reduce friction while Roamly proves that it can save the organizer time and effort.)_
- **Pricing model (The model):** _(Outcome/transaction-based pricing tied to completed group bookings, rather than charging per user or per seat.)_
- **Who pays (Buyer vs. user):** _(The group organizer initiates the booking, but each traveler pays their share. Roamly can collect the group service fee as part of the booking transaction.)_
- **Packaging (The structure):** _(Keep core group coordination included with the booking and monetize through the booking transaction rather than putting basic group functionality behind a subscription.)_
- **Tiers (What each is for):** _(Standard Groups: Small friend/family groups with shared booking, split payments, and itinerary.
Large Groups: Larger celebrations/reunions with additional coordination needs.
Custom Groups: Larger or more complex group experiences requiring host support.)_
- **Which research method (Van Westendorp, Max-Diff, Conjoint) would you commission, and what specific assumption are you trying to validate?:** _(Conjoint. I would use it to validate the assumption that travelers will choose a slightly higher-priced Roamly booking in exchange for easier group coordination, split payments, and shared planning.
- )_
- **What is the one thing in your recommendation that would change everything if you got it wrong?:** _(Willingness to pay. If organizers don't see enough value in the coordination benefits to accept even a small additional fee, we'd need to rethink the monetization model.)_
